Predict Recovery Rate

Enter health and demographic metrics to predict recovery probability and improve decision-making

Key Features of Our Prediction System

Income (USD)

The average income of the patient in USD. Higher income often leads to better access to healthcare, improving recovery outcomes.

Year

The year during which the patient received hospital care. Reflects advancements in medical technology and healthcare policies.

DALYs

Disability-Adjusted Life Years measure disease burden. A higher DALY indicates greater disease burden, reducing recovery rates.

Mortality Rate

The percentage of affected population that dies from disease. Higher rates suggest more severe disease, lowering recovery rates.

Prevalence Rate

The percentage of population affected by disease. Higher prevalence may strain healthcare resources, impacting recovery.

Doctors per 1000

Number of doctors per 1000 people. Higher ratios mean better access to healthcare and more personalized treatment.

Urbanization Rate

Percentage in urban areas. Urban areas typically have better healthcare facilities and infrastructure.

Health Access

Percentage with healthcare access. Critical for recovery rates as timely intervention improves outcomes.

Average Cost

Average treatment cost in USD. Higher costs may be a barrier, negatively affecting recovery rates.

Beds per 1000

Hospital beds per 1000 people. More beds mean better capacity to accommodate patients.

Disease Information

The primary viral disease analyzed. Different diseases have distinct recovery rates.

Country Information

The patient's country. Recovery rates are influenced by country-specific healthcare systems and policies.
